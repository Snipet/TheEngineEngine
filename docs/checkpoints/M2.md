# Checkpoint M2: the listening pack

*Package X's page (the whole program under test). Nobody in the cloud sessions can hear: these files are for your ears.
Each group compares one thing; the files of a group are rendered by the same scenario and matched in loudness, so a
difference you hear is the model's, not the level's.*

## Making the pack (about 40 minutes for the whole pack on one core; `--quick` about 10)

From a checkout of the branch `claude/awesome-gates-ntdb58` (the scenarios are in `tests/sim/combo/scenarios/`), with
either a build of your own or the CI artefact's tools:

```sh
# macOS or Linux, from a build tree (cmake --workflow --preset release-verify, or the dev preset)
scripts/listening-pack.sh --quick            # the ten most telling files, out/listening-pack-quick/
scripts/listening-pack.sh                    # every group, out/listening-pack/

# from CI's artefact (tee-macos-arm64), unpacked beside the checkout
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/tee-macos-arm64     # once, as at M1
scripts/listening-pack.sh --bin ~/Downloads/tee-macos-arm64 --quick
```

```powershell
# Windows
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts\listening-pack.ps1 -Bin C:\path\to\tee-windows-x64 -Quick
```

The folder holds `<group>-<label>.wav` (32-bit float, 48 kHz, stereo) and `README.txt`: the commit, each group's gain,
and every line that was skipped (a build whose package has not landed yet) or that names fields the program lacks
(then the pair may sound the same: say so rather than judge it). What is rendered is `scripts/listening-pack.tsv`.

**Scenarios** (each the same for every file of a group; times in seconds):
- `tour`: the key at 0.25, running by 2; idle to 5; flat out in neutral 5 to 8.5 (to the limiter); lift; a blip at
  9.3; key off at 11.
- `pull`: start; first on the paddle at 3, half throttle, full at 4.5; second at 6.5, third at 8.5; lift at 10.5, a
  downshift at 11; brakes at 12; clutch down and key off at 14.
- `launch`: start; first at 3 on the brake, 60 % pedal held; the brake let go and full throttle at 5; second at 7.5;
  lift at 9, brake, key off at 11.5.
- `cold`: -20 C; the key held from 0.25 to 4.25; idle; key off at 10.

## Yes or no

Answer each with yes or no and a sentence; a time (seconds into the file) helps.

- [ ] 01: in b, the tyres spin and you hear it (the revs flare) in first, then it hooks up.
- [ ] 02: in b, the shifts sound like a car's (a drop, then the pull again), and the downshift blips.
- [ ] 03: a and b are the same kind of V8; b is no worse.
- [ ] 04: a and b are the same kind of six; b is no worse.
- [ ] 05: b spools and flutters like a turbo (after F lands).
- [ ] 06: b, c and d each differ from a as the table says (after G lands).
- [ ] 07: the idle gets rougher from a to c, and c is the strongest at the top (after E lands).
- [ ] 08: the change-over at 5500 rpm is clear and not a click (after E lands).
- [ ] 09: the bangs follow the dial.
- [ ] 10: the bangs follow the dial, with the new ECU (after H lands).
- [ ] 11 to 15: with every package on, each archetype still sounds like itself; nothing broken.
- [ ] 16: the cold start catches and settles without a bang.

## The ten of `--quick`

01 a and b, 05 a and b, 06 a and c, 07 a and c, 10 c, 12 b: the launch, the turbo, the exhaust, the cam, the bangs and
a whole machine with everything on.

## What was measured before you listen (not judged)

Every file of the pack is also run by `tee-tests-combo`: finite, within full scale, no DC above 0.005 of full scale,
the largest sample step within twice the reference's own on the same build and scenario, it starts, idles between
450 and 1800 rpm (a diesel 350 to 1500), revs, moves, stops; the numbers are in
[docs/measurements/combo/](../measurements/combo/). Open findings that a file may show: `tests/sim/combo/Known.h`.
