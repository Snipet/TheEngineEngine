# For the owner: Claude Desktop with the window minimised (Mac), 27 Nov

**What to do.** Use an app built from the branch at 3e35f7a9 or later, or from `main` once it has moved past it. In
the app's SETTINGS, turn **Allow Claude Desktop** on (with TheEngineEngine's extension installed in Claude Desktop),
open any build, turn the key and let the engine idle. Then minimise the window (the yellow button, or Cmd-M) so that
it is in the Dock, not merely behind another window, and in Claude Desktop ask two things, one after the other: first
"which build is open in TheEngineEngine?", then one change you would hear at idle, in your own words (for example
"raise the idle speed a little"). When both are answered, restore the window from the Dock.

**What you should see and hear, and what to tell us if you don't.** Both answers should come back within a few
seconds while the window stays in the Dock, and the change should be heard straight away, before you restore the
window; once restored, the window shows the new value and Undo takes it back. If an answer takes long or fails, tell us
which of the two it was, roughly how many seconds it took, and Claude Desktop's words exactly (the one we are guarding
against reads "timed out before the app took it: nothing was done"); if the answer came but the sound did not change
until you restored the window, say that; and in either case say whether the window was in the Dock or hidden some
other way (another Space, full screen), your macOS version, and the app's version line (TheEngineEngine --version, or
the About box).
