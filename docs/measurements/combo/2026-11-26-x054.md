# X-054: the judge is blind to a single-sample click where its neighbourhood is loud (26 Nov; owner X, with the lead)

**First lines.** On the REFERENCE sound path (sound.model off: every combo row without sound.model, and every
reference), a single-sample click about 11 dB over the firing's sharpest edge passes today's judge in 7 of 17 copies on
a steady full-throttle stretch (the small four's pull), and in 17 of 17 at the limiter (the small four's tour; 14 of 17
on the turbo six's). With sound.model on (the path every shipped build runs) the same click is caught in 17 of 17 on
the steady stretch. A "0 of 17" outside an event on the reference path is not proof of no click.

**Why** (tests/sim/combo/Combo.cpp and host/src/Measures.cpp:159, largestStep). A sample's measure is its difference d
over the RMS of the differences within +-10 ms (480 samples at 48 kHz), leaving out only d itself. A single-sample impulse
has two adjacent differences, +A and -A; each edge's level contains the other, so the measure tends to sqrt(960) = 31 as
A grows: it saturates (measured: 1.0 and 3.0 read the same windows to the digit). The excess (excessAt) is that
saturated measure in the click's firing period over the median of the same measure in the periods +-kT: where the
firings themselves read 9 to 12 (full throttle on the reference path, whose 0.85 output limiter squares the edges) it
tops out near 2.5 to 2.7, under a bound near 2.8; where the neighbours are quiet (a start, idle, a shift's cut) the same
click reads 4 and is caught. The step rule does see it (26.9 over 20.7) but is silenced where the excess is defined (the
ruling of 25 Nov 17:24), and is itself capped near 31.

**A floor of the method.** In some copies the reference path's own firing edges reach about 2 per sample (a full swing
in one sample), so a 3.0 click stands only about 3.5 dB over them: no measure that judges a click against its
neighbouring firings can see it (the level taken only from the neighbours, "raw", still misses 1 to 2 of 17).
