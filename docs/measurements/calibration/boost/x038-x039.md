Every one of the four is needed: boost.model 1, the recirculating blow-off (kind 2), the small charge volume, and the
oversized blower.

The last good sample against the first outside 2 kPa-1 MPa (a scratch latch, during the crank):

| | t s | v1.p Pa | v2.p Pa | p1 Pa | mInlet kg/s | mBov kg/s | bovAt |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| last good | 0.196966 | 555,730 | 555,644 | 50,662 (the 0.5 pOut floor) | -0.190 | 0.035 | 1.00 |
| first bad | 0.196977 | 557,514 | 555,786 | 1,000,210 | +0.286 | 0 | 1.00 |

**Measured:**
- The 4 L blower on 50 cc holds 5.5 bar in the 0.5 L of pipes while the engine cranks.
- The blow-off stands wide open (bovAt 1.0) and returns its flow to the compressor's inlet.
- The filter's flow has reversed (mInlet -0.19 kg/s) with p1 on its floor.
- In one sample p1 jumps to 1.0 MPa and mInlet flips sign.

**Suspected, not proven:** the inlet is an algebraic node with no volume, p1 = pOut - kFilter m|m|, bounded below at
0.5 pOut and not above. It is evaluated on the last sample's flow. When the recirculated flow exceeds the blower's
draw, the filter's flow reverses, p1 rises with m squared, the blow-off's flow (into p1) shuts, the flow reverses
back, and so on: an explicit iteration whose loop gain (2 kFilter |m| times the flows' slope against p1) is far over
one when the blower is 80 times the engine. The kIn term in the blow-off's step linearises only that one flow. The
same family as X-026 (the inlet's slope), which I bounded below.

**Cures to weigh (none built; sim/ is frozen):**
- (a) Solve the inlet node implicitly each sample: the blower's draw, the compressors' and the blow-off's return all
  as functions of p1, by a few bisection steps between 0.5 pOut and the highest pressure feeding it.
- (b) Give the inlet a small volume (the filter box), making it a state like v1 and v2.
- (c) Bound p1 above by the highest pressure feeding it, max(pOut, v2.p). That is the mirror of X-026's floor, cheap,
  but a bound, not the physics.

I would take (a): it is the physics as the model states it, with no new number.
