# The 2.0 turbo four's real engine: the EA888 gen 3 (Mk7 GTI), what the sources give

*Looked up 24 Nov for the lead's ruling (22:49, 23:13): the first-launch engine held to a real one. Each figure with its source and kind. The build is not changed from these until the model's parts land (N1's pressure-reading phaser schedule, N2's published turbine maps): this is the record to build from then.*

**For the top end (the lead's item 2):** the owner's log reads 11.8 psi (81 kPa) at 6048 rpm and 11 psi (76 kPa) at 6400; the build's boost map holds 81 kPa at 6200. So by the one source found, the build's top-end boost is the real engine's, and the 276 N m at 6200 against the 250 that the maker's 162 kW means is the model's at that boost, not the taper's. (A pull is transient: steady-state boost at 6200 may differ; no steady figure was found.)
