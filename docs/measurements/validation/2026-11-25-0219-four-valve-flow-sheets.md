Found by a web search on 25 Nov. **None names the depression, the valve sizes and the bench in one place.** CFM, intake / exhaust; lift in inches (0.050 in = 1.27 mm). Basis per source.

- **A2 Nissan SR20DET, stock**, "the same testing method".
