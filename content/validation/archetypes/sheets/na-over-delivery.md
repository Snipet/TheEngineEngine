# The naturally aspirated over-delivery: seven engines, build beside the real one

*For card N3. Written by `content/validation/archetypes/sheets/make.py` from the builds (`content/builds/archetypes/`) and the newest table (`docs/manual/validation.md`, commit 30d2dfc). The real engine's figures: SEARCH, confirmed by a web search on 2026-11-24 (the sources named); MEMORY, recalled and not looked up, firm as said. Every target is at the crank and net (SAE J1349 or DIN 70020: with the engine's own exhaust, intake and accessories), never at the wheels.*

## Air-cooled flat six (`air-cooled-flat-six-36`)

Stands for the 1989-93 3.6 L air-cooled flat six (M64/01, 964 Carrera 2/4). **Target basis:** 184 kW (250 PS DIN) at 6100 rpm, 310 N m at 4800: DIN net at the crank, the European rating (SEARCH: Wikipedia, auto-data.net); the US SAE net rating is 247 hp (184 kW): the same.

**Measured (the table):** power 238 kW (180 kW to 195 kW) **miss**; torque 410 N m (300 N m to 320 N m) **miss**.

| | The build | The real engine |
|---|---|---|
| Bore, stroke, compression | 100.0 x 76.4 mm, 3600 cc, 11.3:1 (rod 127.0 mm) | 100 x 76.4 mm, 3600 cc, 11.3:1 (SEARCH) |
| Head | 2 valves, Rocker train, ports 1.35/1.5 (quality: 1.35/1.5 the default, a stock 232 CFM small-block head at 1.35) | two valves a cylinder, one cam a bank by chain, rocker arms, twin spark plugs (MEMORY) |
| Valves | intake 49.0 mm, exhaust 42.5 mm | intake 49 mm, exhaust 42.5 mm (MEMORY, to 1 mm) |
| Cams | intake 256/222 deg (seat/1 mm), 11.5 mm, centre 108; exhaust 252/216, 10.5 mm, centre 112 | mild road cam; published duration not to hand (MEMORY: about 240 deg at the seat) |
| Intake | runner 0.53 m; one throttle and a plenum | a resonance intake with a switching flap (the 964's two-stage plenum) (MEMORY) |
| Exhaust | converter [1, 1], mufflers [2, 2], sides 2 | (a road system with converters) |
| Springs, redline | 48500 N/m over 303 N; 6700 rpm | |

*Note:* dry sump; air cooled: the head and barrels run hotter than a water-cooled engine's.
